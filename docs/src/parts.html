Nero/overview

Five pieces. One Rust core.

Use whichever ones annoy you the most. Or all five.
1
keep-onscreen
Every new window fits its monitor. Period.
2
tilingshell-patch
Tiling that behaves like you thought it did.
3
nero-layout
Log back in. Every window is where you left it.
4
nero-workspaces
Every monitor gets its own workspaces.
5
taskbar
A proper taskbar, with a system monitor in it.
